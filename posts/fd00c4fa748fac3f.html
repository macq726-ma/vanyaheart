五、Verification methods, just follow them

For certificates, take the number to the National Medical Products Administration's "Cosmetics Production License Information" or the provincial bureau platform to check, and verify the three items of company name, address, and licensed items; only when all three match does it count.

The official verification entry is nmpa.gov.cn: go to "Cosmetics Production License Information" and search by company name, comparing the three items of name, production address, and licensed items one by one; for filing information, search by filing number under the National Medical Products Administration's "Cosmetics Filing Information."

For filing, ask the factory for the filing number of the category you are preparing to make, and check the ingredients and efficacy claims yourself; do not just look at the screenshot provided by the factory.

For a factory audit, look at only three places: the cleanroom differential pressure gauge of the filling line, the batch identification in the raw material warehouse, and the retention sample records in the retention sample room. These three places cannot be faked; everything else can be staged. For capacity, do not look at the PPT; look at the current month's production schedule and work orders in production.

六、Clarification of common misunderstandings

There is a saying on the internet that "a small OEM factory's certificate is just a piece of waste paper." Upon verification, this mostly originates from individual cases from an earlier batch of small workshops that only did filling and did not do filing, and is inconsistent with the current situation. The way to judge now is simple: take the license number to the provincial bureau platform and check; if the three items of name, address, and licensed items can be found, it is a valid certificate; if it cannot be found, nothing else matters.

There is also the saying that "low MOQ necessarily means poor quality," which comes from the early stage when small batches could only be filled manually. In fact, MOQ and quality have no causal relationship; what determines quality is the three items of workshop grade, retention sample system, and testing frequency. Small batches can also run on fully automatic lines; it is just that the packaging material MOQ will raise the per-unit cost.

There is also the saying that "a low quote means low total cost." The total cost is the sum of the three items of sampling rounds, rework rate, and packaging material loss; the line on the quotation sheet is only the beginning. For the same foundation liquid, a 15% difference in quotes is common, but the cost of one rework usually exceeds that difference.
