FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about working with us.

What is the minimum order quantity (MOQ)?
MOQ is 2000 units for existing formulas. Custom formula development starts at 1000 units. We understand indie brands need flexibility — we'll find a quantity that works for you.
Can you customize the formula for my market?
Absolutely. We adjust texture, coverage, finish (matte/dewy/satin), shade, fragrance, and active ingredients. Send us a reference product and we can reverse-engineer it.
What certifications do you have?
ISO 22716 (GMPC) certified. FDA registered for US market (MoCRA compliant). EU CPNP ready. Halal certification available. MSDS and COA provided with every shipment.
How long does production and shipping take?
Production: 7–15 business days after sample approval. Custom R&D adds 5–10 days. Shipping: 3–7 days by air (DHL/FedEx), 20–35 days by sea freight.
Can you do custom packaging and branding?
Yes — full packaging customization: bottles, labels, boxes, insert cards. We source packaging from our network or work with your designs. Complete turn-key branding solution.
What payment methods do you accept?
T/T bank transfer, L/C, PayPal (for samples), and Wise. Standard terms: 30% deposit to start production, 70% balance before shipment.
